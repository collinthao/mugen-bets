-< Fatals >--<===================================================================>-

Fatality              : ~D, DF, F, D, DB, B, x+y / ~D, DF, F, D, DB, B, a+b

Note: Fatalities can only happen after the first round, if the criterias are met.
Aka the enemy health is below or equal 10%. When a fatality can happen, a helper will indicates the player.
When you successfully did the combo, you can decide which fatality will occour by holding left, right, up, or down during this state.
If you don't press and hold any button, the system will choose randomly. The only exceptions are the character specific fatalities.
