Controls
========

Basic Controls
--------------

Son is a 6-button character. Button X is his weak
punch, button Y his medium punch, button Z his strong
punch, A his weak kick, B his medium kick,
and C for his strong kick.

We will use the notation:
D - down
F - forward
U - up
B - back
x - button X (weak punch)
y - button Y (medium punch)
z - button Z (strong punch)
a - button A (weak kick)
b - button B (medium kick)
c - button C (strong kick)


Standard Movement
-----------------

U              - Jump
UF             - Jump forwards
F              - Walk forwards
DF             - Crouch
D              - Crouch
DB             - Crouch or low guard
B              - Walk backwards or high guard
UB             - Jump back
F, F           - Forward dash
B, B           - Back dash
xy             - Recovery (when falling)
F, F           - Forward dash (when air)
B, B           - Back dash (when air)
xy/yz/xz       - Guard Push (while blocking)
Hold F/B       - Rolling Recovery (when lying down)

Basic Standing Attacks
----------------------

x              - L. Punch
y              - M. Punch
z              - S. Punch
a              - W. Kick
b              - M. Kick
c              - S. Kick

Basic Crouching Attacks
-----------------------

x              - Low W. Punch
y              - Low M. Punch
z              - Launcher
a              - Low W. Kick
b              - Low M. Kick
c              - Low S. Kick

Basic Air Attacks
-----------------

x              - Air W. Punch
y              - Air M. Punch
z              - Meteor
a              - Air W. Kick
b              - Air M. Kick
c              - Air S. Kick

Throws
------

F + z (when close) - Throw

Special Attacks
---------------

D, DF, F, x/y    - Knife swing

D, DB, B, x/y    - Dive Tackle (Air OK)

D, DF, F, a/b    - Chain

D, DB, B, a/b    - Divebomb (Air OK)

Hyper Attacks
-------------

D, DF, F, x+y  - Raging Jab (use 1 power bar)
D, DB, B, x+y  - Gas Cylinder Throw (Air OK) (use 1/2 power bar)
D, DB, B, a+b  - Unstoppable Rage (use 2 power bar)
